Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions

Have a question not listed here? Call our clinic desk directly at +91 80 4122 8900.

Will dental procedures hurt?

We apply a topical anesthetic gel first and deliver local anesthesia using The Wand® computer-assisted system at a slow, regulated flow rate that patients barely register. You can raise your hand to pause at any point during your appointment.

Do you accept health insurance & cashless mediclaim?

Yes. We have cashless affiliations with major corporate and health insurance networks, and we assist you in filing claims for OPD dental coverage. Bring your insurance e-card and we verify eligibility prior to beginning treatment.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call our hotline immediately at +91 80 4122 8900. We reserve dedicated emergency slots every single day (including Sundays) for sudden toothache, trauma, broken restorations, or chipped teeth.

Is there parking available at the studio?

Yes. We have two reserved parking slots directly in front of the studio building, plus valet parking on 100 Feet Road. If coming by metro, Indiranagar Metro Station is just a 7-minute walk (500m).

Can I pay in interest-free monthly instalments?

Yes. We offer 0% interest EMI tenures of 3, 6, 9, or 12 months on all procedures above ₹10,000 in partnership with major credit cards and healthcare finance providers.

Have a Dental Emergency?

Severe toothache, broken tooth, or swelling? We have same-day emergency slots available now.
